Lab 6: Passive RC Filters · RC Low-Pass and RC High-Pass

Lab 6 setup

Both filters use R = 1 kΩ and C = 10 nF. For the low-pass filter, measure the output across the capacitor; for the high-pass filter, measure it across the resistor.

RC LPF · output across CRC HPF · output across RTheoretical cutoff · 15.92 kHz

Gain is calculated from the measured voltages: 20 log₁₀(Vout,pp / Vin,pp). Connect CH1 to the input and CH2 to the output. For phase, select Phase(2,1) (CH2 minus CH1); the reverse channel order reverses the sign. Near cutoff, expect about −45° for LPF and +45° for HPF.

Important: The downloadable template lists the handout's 19 frequencies (1 kHz through 400 kHz). The plotter does not require every row to be present or filled. It plots each valid measurement and skips blanks without creating values. Your LPF can be plotted with 18 measured points.

Cutoff frequency estimates

Measured cutoff is estimated by a −3.0103 dB crossing relative to the measured passband level, using interpolation on logarithmic frequency. The passband reference is the median of up to three lowest-frequency LPF or highest-frequency HPF measurements.

Bode plots

Each filter and response is shown separately. Missing measurements are not drawn or imputed.

Processed measurements
