Lab 6 · Passive RC Low-Pass and High-Pass Filters

Lab 6 measurement setup

Both circuits use R = 1 kΩ and C = 10 nF, giving a theoretical cutoff of 15.92 kHz. RC LPF output is across the capacitor; RC HPF output is across the resistor.

Measure the actual input and output peak-to-peak voltages at each frequency. Gain (dB) = 20 log₁₀(Vout,pp / Vin,pp). Use CH1 = input, CH2 = output, and select Phase(2,1) (2 first, then 1). Expected phase near cutoff: LPF −45° and HPF +45°.

The template has 19 frequencies from 1 kHz to 400 kHz in each of two independent CSV tables. A complete magnitude or phase series can be plotted independently. Blank columns are allowed, but partially completed required columns cannot be plotted.

Cutoff frequency estimates

Measured cutoff is estimated from the passband level (median of the first three LPF or last three HPF gain measurements) minus 3.0103 dB, with interpolation in log-frequency. The theoretical cutoff is always 15.92 kHz for real lab data.
