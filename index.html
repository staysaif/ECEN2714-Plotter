How to use this tool

1. Record dataEnter your measured frequency, input/output Vpp, and phase values in the CSV template.
2. Upload CSVChoose or drag your CSV file into this page. Your file is processed locally in your browser.
3. Check crossingsUse the preliminary plots to identify where extra refinement measurements are needed.
4. Export figuresUpload the updated CSV and download the final magnitude and phase plots for your report.

Upload measurement data

Frequency_Hz is required. Recommended measurement columns are Vin_Vpp, Vout_Inv_Vpp, Phase_Inv_deg, Vout_NonInv_Vpp, and Phase_NonInv_deg. If optional columns are missing, the tool will plot whatever valid data are available.
